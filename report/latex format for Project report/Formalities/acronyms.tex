\begin{center}
    \Large \textbf{List of Principal Symbols and Acronyms}
\end{center}

\vspace{1.5em}
\begin{description}
  \item[AP]      Average Precision over a ranked list for a single query.
  \item[BGE]     BAAI General Embedding --- a family of sentence embedding models by the Beijing Academy of AI.
  \item[BM25]    Best Matching 25 --- a probabilistic term-frequency based lexical ranking function.
  \item[CLEF]    Conference and Labs of the Evaluation Forum --- annual information retrieval evaluation campaign.
  \item[CV]      Cross-Validation --- model evaluation technique that partitions training data into folds.
  \item[FAISS]   Facebook AI Similarity Search --- a library for efficient approximate nearest-neighbor search in dense vector spaces.
  \item[IDF]     Inverse Document Frequency --- a measure of how rare a term is across the corpus.
  \item[IR]      Information Retrieval --- the task of finding relevant documents in a collection.
  \item[JOKER]   Wordplay \& Humour Modelling --- the CLEF lab focused on humor-aware NLP and retrieval.
  \item[L2R]     Learning to Rank --- a supervised approach to optimizing document ranking.
  \item[MAP]     Mean Average Precision --- the mean of per-query Average Precision values; primary metric at CLEF.
  \item[MRR]     Mean Reciprocal Rank --- average of the reciprocal rank of the first relevant result per query.
  \item[NDCG]    Normalized Discounted Cumulative Gain --- a rank-position-sensitive retrieval metric.
  \item[NLP]     Natural Language Processing.
  \item[P@10]    Precision at 10 --- fraction of the top-10 ranked documents that are relevant.
  \item[PRF]     Pseudo-Relevance Feedback --- query expansion using vocabulary from the initial top results.
  \item[RM3]     Relevance Model 3 --- a specific PRF formulation based on Lavrenko \& Croft's relevance models.
  \item[RoBERTa] Robustly Optimized BERT Pretraining Approach --- a pre-trained transformer language model.
  \item[RRF]     Reciprocal Rank Fusion --- a parameter-free method for combining ranked lists.
  \item[SOTA]    State of the Art --- the best published result on a given benchmark.
  \item[TF-IDF]  Term Frequency -- Inverse Document Frequency --- a classical term-weighting scheme.
  \item[TREC]    Text Retrieval Conference --- the standard format for ranked retrieval evaluation.
  \item[VRAM]    Video RAM --- memory on a GPU device.
  \item[XGBoost] Extreme Gradient Boosting --- a scalable, regularized gradient-boosted tree ensemble.
\end{description}
